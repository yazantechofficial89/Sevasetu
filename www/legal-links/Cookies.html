Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit such as login details, preferences, and user behavior.

2. How We Use Cookies

SevaSetu uses cookies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and ensure the platform works properly for all users.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies – Required for the website to function properly.

Analytics Cookies – Help us understand how users interact.

Preference Cookies – Store user preferences.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time.

6. Contact Us

Contact the SevaSetu support team for queries.
